The Walker Lab studies how enzymes achieve selectivity, and how that selectivity can be redirected toward new chemistry.

Biosynthetic enzymes routinely perform transformations that are difficult to accomplish synthetically, acting on a single site of a complex substrate with control over regiochemistry and stereochemistry. Understanding the structural and mechanistic basis for that control is a prerequisite for engineering it. Our work combines biochemical characterization, structural analysis, and directed evolution to map how selectivity is encoded and to build enzymes with altered specificity for biosynthetic applications.
